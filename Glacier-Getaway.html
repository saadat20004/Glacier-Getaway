Before you go

Quick answers

Still have a question? Add it to your booking request and the hosts will reply.

Will my RV or trailer fit?

Sites are for vehicles under 24 ft. The access road is dirt with some turns, so smaller rigs reach the shoreline spots most easily.

Is there cell service or WiFi?

Cell signal can be weak in the area, but WiFi is available on site so you can stay connected.

Are showers and toilets available?

Yes. There is a gender neutral shower house with hot water, plus toilets and potable water on site.

Can I have a campfire?

Campfires are allowed. Smokeless solo fire pits and propane fire pits are available, and firewood can be bought on site.

Can I bring my dog?

Pets are welcome. Please keep them close, as this is wild country with deer, elk, moose and the occasional grizzly.

Is the gear really free to use?

Yes. Kayaks, the canoe, paddle boat, fishing boat, rods, lures and the BBQ are shared with all guests on a first come basis.
